- Whenever you give a solution, a plan or next steps that I or another session will carry out, say which model fits, with a short reason. If the stages of a plan differ in difficulty, give one per stage.
- Match the model to the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Typical work | Claude | Codex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hard | New design or architecture, unclear requirements, tricky debugging, changes across many modules, security, data migrations, final review of important work | Strongest (Opus) | Strongest model, high reasoning effort |
