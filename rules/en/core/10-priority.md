
## Priority of information and decisions

1. Explicit instructions from me in the current task.
2. Confirmed requirements, acceptance criteria and decisions I have approved.
3. Behaviour proven by the current code, configuration and automated tests.
4. Technical notes, README, implementation plans and release checklists.
5. Your own inferences and suggestions.

Always distinguish “current implementation”, “target requirement”, “proposal” and “unconfirmed assumption”. A target document does not mean something is implemented; code and tests prove current behaviour but must not silently rewrite confirmed requirements. When they conflict, describe the conflict, its impact and the options first.
