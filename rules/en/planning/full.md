
## Plan first, then build

- Turn a new idea into a complete plan (idea brief, PRD, prototype, technical design, development plan) before implementing; use the `idea-to-plan` skill.
- Small changes don't need the full process, but still state the scope and the done-criteria first.
- Before a change that touches more than one file, check it against the plan in the progress summary; if it isn't in the plan, confirm with me first.
- Move one stage at a time; stop after each stage for my confirmation.
